Word-frequency data in this directory is derived from wordfreq (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq)
by Robyn Speer, which compiles frequencies from Wikipedia, SUBTLEX, OpenSubtitles, Google Books Ngrams,
the Leeds Internet Corpus, Twitter, Reddit and other sources (see the wordfreq README for full credits).

scripts/build-freq.py) are shared under the same license.
